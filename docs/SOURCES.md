# Rule sources

The implementation follows the advanced rules of the 2022 commercial *HUNTER×HUNTER Gungi* game. Source PDFs and images are not included in the distribution.

| ID | Source | Coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | [Japanese product rulebook](https://planetbanatt.net/docs/gungi_rulebook_jp.pdf) | Third-party scan: movement, deployment, captures, drops, betrayal, and game endings |
| R3 | [UNIVERSAL MUSIC JAPAN official Q&A](https://www.universal-music.co.jp/other/hunter-gungi__qa/) | Rule clarifications and corrections to the rulebook |
| R4 | [Official rules overview](https://www.universal-music.co.jp/cinema/hunter-gungi/) | Advanced deployment and turn order |
| R5 | [Official Q&A archer diagrams](https://www.universal-music.co.jp/other/hunter-gungi__qa/) | Archer paths and blocking by taller towers; diagrams 1–7 in display order |

The all-or-nothing betrayal rule and the treatment of stalemate in [RULES.md](../RULES.md) are interpretations adopted by this engine, not additional official rulings.
